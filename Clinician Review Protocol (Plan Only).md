# Clinician Review Protocol (Plan Only)

This is a proposed protocol, not completed clinical validation. No clinical
validation has been performed.

1. Obtain ethics/IRB and data-governance approval, documented consent, and
   minimum-necessary de-identification before data access.
2. Recruit appropriately qualified clinicians and define inclusion/exclusion
   criteria before reviewing any outcomes.
3. Use a blinded, representative sample with a locked model/ruleset version.
   Record clinician assessment independently and measure inter-rater agreement
   (Cohen's kappa with confidence intervals).
4. Prespecify sample-size justification, operating threshold, acceptable
   sensitivity/specificity trade-offs, calibration criteria, subgroup analyses,
   missing-data handling, and adverse-outcome escalation.
5. Separate retrospective development, locked evaluation, and prospective
   validation. Do not tune on the locked test set.
6. Stop review for safety signals, unexpected subgroup harms, or consent/data
   governance breaches. Report all results, including failures, before any
   clinical claim.
